# Templates

Three working documents for the start of a local validation. They are written for the vendor's deterioration score at Tonmick Health, so that every blank has a worked example; replace the example with your own. Each maps to a chapter of the book.

| Template | Use it when | Chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `scoping_questions.md` | Someone asks "can you check this AI tool?" and you need to know what is being asked | 1 |
| `data_request.md` | You know the question and need the extract | 6 |
| `validation_protocol.md` | You have the data and must fix the analysis before looking at results | 6 |
